Hi, I'm **Prince Singh**, a software engineer who works mostly with Java and web backends. I spend a lot of my time working out *why* a system behaves the way it does, and this blog is where I write those answers down.

## Why this blog exists

Most concepts I thought I understood only became solid when I tried to explain them in writing. Sudo Explain is where I do that in public, so that the next person with the same doubt can find the answer faster than I did.

## What you'll find here

- **Core concepts:** the fundamentals that tutorials skip past
- **System design:** how real systems are put together, and the trade-offs behind them
- **AI:** what it does, how it works, and where it fits into software work
- **Problems I ran into:** bugs, dead ends and the fixes that finally worked

## How I write

Every post follows the same shape:

1. **The doubt:** the question that confused me.
2. **The misconception:** what I assumed, and why it was wrong.
3. **How it actually works:** the mechanism, with the smallest example I can build.
4. **The takeaway:** the one thing worth remembering.

I try to keep examples small enough to run, and I say plainly when something is a simplification.
