I learn best when I have to explain something to someone else. This blog is that someone else.

Each post follows one pattern:

1. **The doubt.** The question that confused me.
2. **The misconception.** What I assumed, and why it was wrong.
3. **How it actually works.** The mechanism, with the smallest example I can build.
4. **The takeaway.** One thing worth remembering.

## What this site is made of

Nothing exotic. Plain HTML and CSS, generated from Markdown by a ~500-line script with no dependencies. No trackers, no cookie banners, no web fonts to download.

> If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough.

If something here is wrong or unclear, tell me. A correction is a gift.
